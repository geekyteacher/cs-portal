Bronze: Concert Gatekeeper

Allow entry only when the person is 18 or older and has a ticket. Fill in both gaps. 

age = int(input("Age: "))
has_ticket = input("Do you have a ticket? (yes/no): ").strip().lower()

if age >= _____ and has_ticket == "_____":
    print("Entry Granted")
else:
    print("Entry Denied")

Hint: the age limit is 18. The ticket response is text.
