## Key ideas

Sound is energy that travels in waves through air, water, or other substances, and can be heard. (Collins English Dictionary, 2020)

For us to capture or record sounds we need a microphone, and analogue to digital convertor and a device to process and store the data. 

![Sound capture process: a microphone converts air-pressure changes into an electrical signal, an analogue-to-digital converter turns it into digital data, and a computer displays the waveform.](/media/sound-recording-process.jpeg)

Going from analogue to digital we need:
* Sample rate 
* Bit depth (or sampling resolution)
* Bit rate

### Sample Rate:
When converting the analogue wave to digital we take a sample of the waves amplitude at various points in time. This is usually done multiple times per second. This is known as the **sampling rate** and this is measured in **Hertz (Hz)**. 

A higher sample rate means more data is captured leading to a higher quality audio file.
