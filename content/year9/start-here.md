# Year 9 Computing

## Python: from quizzes to projects

This sequence develops Python programs from a familiar starting point: a quiz.
You will practise input, variables, selection, loops, testing, and evaluation,
then use those skills in a project of your choice.

Students may have met some of these ideas already. Start with **Lesson 4:
Improve Your Quiz** to check what you can do. It has a core route that does not
require lists or loops, plus extensions for students who are ready. Lessons 5
onwards teach and practise loops so that everyone can work towards the same
assessment and project criteria.

## Shared project goals

By the end of the sequence, your program should use:

- input and variables;
- selection with `if` and, where useful, `elif` and `else`;
- a loop that repeats a meaningful part of the program; and
- testing to check normal, boundary, and incorrect inputs.

Use the lesson support and extension tasks to work at the right pace. You do
not need to have studied loops or lists before Lesson 4.
