# Maze Collector: Build a Scratch Maze Game

Build a single-screen maze game. Guide a character through the maze, collect
items, and reach the exit. Start with the core game, then choose challenges
that make it your own.

## What you will practise

- using X and Y coordinates to move a sprite;
- using a `forever` loop to keep checking the controls;
- using conditions to respond to keys and collisions;
- using a variable to keep score;
- testing, debugging, and improving a game.

## The six lessons

1. [Design the maze and move the player](./L01-Maze-Map-and-Movement)
2. [Add walls and a finish](./L02-Walls-and-Finish)
3. [Collect items and keep score](./L03-Collectibles-and-Score)
4. [Create a win condition](./L04-Win-Conditions-and-Timer)
5. [Playtest and improve](./L05-Playtest-and-Improve)
6. [Showcase and evaluate](./L06-Showcase-and-Evaluation)

## The core game

By the end, aim to make a game where the player can:

1. move through a maze without passing through its walls;
2. collect three items; and
3. avoid one patrol enemy; and
4. reach the exit to win.

Extra enemies, timers, hazards, extra levels, sound, and animation are
optional challenges. Choose one improvement at a time and test it before
adding another.
