## Today you will

Showcase your Maze Collector game, try other games respectfully, and explain
one thing you tested or improved.

## Prepare your game

Before sharing, check that:

- the game starts with the green flag;
- the player begins at the start;
- all four controls work;
- walls stop the player;
- collectibles update the score once;
- the patrol enemy sends the player back to the start when touched;
- the exit checks the score and shows the win message.

If one feature is not finished, be ready to explain what you have tested and
what you would improve next.

## Arcade showcase

Take turns playing classmates' games. Ask before changing or restarting
someone else's project. Leave one specific, kind comment:

- One thing that worked well was...
- One part that was clear or fun was...
- One question or suggestion I have is...

## Self-evaluation

Complete these sentences:

- My game is called...
- The player wins by...
- The enemy behaves by...
- One Scratch script I am proud of is...
- One test I carried out was...
- One problem I fixed or improvement I made was...
- If I had another lesson, I would...

## Optional awards

Celebrate different kinds of success, such as:

- clearest instructions;
- most imaginative maze;
- smoothest controls;
- best playtest improvement;
- most interesting extra feature.

The awards recognise different strengths; a game does not need lots of
features to be successful.

## Share and submit

Save and share your Scratch project using your teacher's instructions. Test
that the shared project link opens before submitting it.
